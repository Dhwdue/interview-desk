# Riley Morgan's practice session

Riley Morgan and Alder Parcel are fictional. This example shows the kind of feedback Interview Desk saves.

## Question

Tell me about a time you found a problem others had missed.

## Answer

I compared the dispatch log with our delivery dashboard and found five missed stops that showed as complete. I took the discrepancy to the shift lead. We updated the dashboard to use the dispatch log, and the next week's handoffs stopped carrying duplicate updates.

## Feedback

The answer explains what Riley checked, who acted, and what changed. On the next attempt, describe how the team verified that the two records matched after the update.
